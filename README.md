# Geospatial Analysis of Carbon Emissions and Urban Environmental Dynamics in Ibadan Metropolis, Nigeria (2018–2025)

**Project Brief**

## 1. Spatial Question

How do atmospheric carbon dioxide (CO2) concentrations vary spatially and temporally across Ibadan Metropolis between 2018 and 2025, and which urban environmental factors  including land use/land cover, vegetation cover, built-up density, land surface temperature, population density, road density, and meteorological conditions are associated with this distribution?

## 2. Study Area

- **Location:** Ibadan Metropolis, Oyo State, Nigeria
- **Study Period:** 2018–2025

## 4. Main Variables

Atmospheric XCO2 (from OCO-2) is the primary carbon variable for this study. Carbon monoxide (CO), methane (CH4) and nitrogen dioxide (NO2) from Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI serve as complementary indicators of urban emission and combustion patterns.

Urban environmental variables include land use/land cover, the Normalised Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), the Normalised Difference Built-up Index (NDBI), land surface temperature, population density, road density, elevation, and meteorological conditions (air temperature, precipitation, relative humidity and wind).

## 5. Proposed Analysis

The datasets will be integrated within a GIS and remote-sensing framework to examine:

- The spatial distribution of atmospheric XCO2 across Ibadan Metropolis.
- Temporal variation in XCO2 concentrations from 2018 to 2025.
- Relationships between XCO2 and urban expansion / built-up density.
- Relationships between XCO2 and vegetation cover.
- Relationships between XCO2 and land surface temperature.
- The influence of population density, road networks, and meteorological conditions on XCO2 distribution.
- Identification of spatial hotspots of elevated atmospheric CO2.
